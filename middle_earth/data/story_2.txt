The Community Responds

Following Elrond's summons, echoes of his call spread throughout Middle-earth. From the hidden corners and distant 
lands, those who heard the call began to respond.

Gandalf, ever watchful, gathered his thoughts. Aragorn, the Ranger, prepared for his role in the journey. Legolas the 
Elf, Gimli the Dwarf, Boromir of Gondor, and Frodo's loyal hobbit companions – Sam, Pippin, and Merry – all 
acknowledged the urgent need for unity.

Their individual responses, though made in isolation, were all directed towards the common goal set forth by Elrond. It 
is your task to ensure their messages of readiness are logged, demonstrating the community's commitment to the quest.
