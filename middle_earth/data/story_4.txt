The Journey Through Moria: Scaling and Load Balancing

Venturing forth, the Fellowship faces the treacherous pass of Caradhras, but ultimately decides to seek passage through 
their ancient, subterranean realm: the Mines of Moria. This vast and perilous network of tunnels presents a daunting 
challenge. To navigate its depths and ensure no one is left behind or overwhelmed, the Fellowship must learn to manage 
their resources efficiently and coordinate their efforts.

In the darkness of Moria, scaling up communication and distributing tasks becomes critical for survival. Your mission 
is to help establish these systems, ensuring the Fellowship can journey through the mines, prepared for whatever might 
lie ahead.
