The Fellowship's First Steps: Establishing a Secure Network

Having departed Rivendell, the nascent Fellowship, guided by Aragorn, now ventures into the wild. Their journey is fraught with peril, and effective communication is paramount. To share vital information and coordinate their movements across potentially hostile lands, they must establish a secure and reliable network.

This secure communication is the first crucial step in their quest, ensuring that messages can be relayed swiftly and safely, no matter the distance or the urgency. Your task is to help build this foundational network, preparing the Fellowship for the challenges ahead.
