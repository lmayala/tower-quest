The Downfall of Isengard

The battle of Helm's Deep is won. The Uruk-hai are scattered, the walls hold, and dawn breaks over
the fortress. But the war is not over — not while Saruman's industry still poisons the land.

Far to the north, the Ents have risen. Treebeard, oldest of the Fangorn shepherds, has led his
kin against Isengard. They have broken the dam and flooded the plain, drowning the furnaces and
workshops that forged Saruman's army. The tower of Orthanc stands alone amid water and ruin, its
master stripped of power.

Now it falls to you to dismantle what remains. Every container, every network, every volume that
served the defense of Helm's Deep must be cleared away — the resources of war released, the
battlefield made clean. Saruman's machinery is broken; yours must be too.

When the last service is gone and the system is pruned, Isengard will trouble Middle-earth no
more. The road ahead leads east, where another fate awaits — but that is a journey for another
day.
