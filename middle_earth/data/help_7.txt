**********************************************************************************
 Stage 7: The Downfall of Isengard
**********************************************************************************

Isengard has fallen. The Ents have broken the dam, drowned the plain, and silenced the forges. All that 
remains of Saruman's works is still and water. Orthanc stands alone.

To finish the game, run the final check script:

 check_isengard_downfall

This will dismantle all of Helm's Deep and release Middle-earth from its burdens.
